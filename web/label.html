The labelled sample

300 comments drawn at random (fixed seed) from every Hacker News comment that links a YouTube video. These labels are the ground truth for the accuracy table. The first pass was written by Claude using the rubric below; change any label you disagree with and the Receipts page re-scores with your version. Your edits stay in this browser until you export them.

Rubric

Recommends: the commenter points readers to the video as worth watching, for example a talk, lecture, documentary, explainer, tutorial or a show they rate. A neutral “here's a good intro” counts. Not a recommendation: a bare link with no context, evidence or citation in an argument, a joke, meme or reaction clip, a music track, a news or political clip, a livestream link, or the commenter's own product or channel.
