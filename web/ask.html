Ask the graph

Ask what to watch. Your question is embedded in the browser, the closest videos become seeds, and the graph pulls in their neighbours: videos linked in the same HN threads, by the same people, or with similar recommendations (structure-aware graph expansion, after SAGE). A small LLM running in your browser writes the answer from that context and cites it.

The first question downloads the embedding model (about 23 MB). Answers need WebGPU and a one-time 1 GB model download; without them you still get the retrieved videos and the graph.
